# Akol privacy policy

_Last updated: [date of publishing]_

Akol ("we", "the app") is a reminder and checklist app for individuals and families. This policy explains what
information the app handles and where it goes. In short: Akol keeps your routines on your phone, it doesn't sell
anything about you, and it doesn't show ads.

## Information stored on your phone

Everything you enter stays on your phone unless you turn on family sync. That includes:

- the names you give family members, and your routines, tasks, times and notes;
- which tasks have been ticked off, and on which days;
- your settings;
- voice recordings you make for voice alarms. Voice recordings are never uploaded. They stay on the phone that
  recorded them. On Android, a copy is kept in the phone's Notifications sound folder so the system can play it as an
  alarm sound.

Reminders and alarms are scheduled on your phone by its own notification system. No server is involved.

**Backups.** If you use "Save a backup", Akol creates text containing your family, routines and tick history, and
hands it to you. Where you keep that text (Notes, email, files) is your choice, and that service's privacy terms apply.
On Android, your phone's own Google backup may also include Akol's data, under Google's terms.

## Family sync (optional)

If you choose **Start family sync** or **Join a family**:

- Your phone signs in anonymously. We don't ask for your name, email address or phone number. A random identifier
  is created so the service knows which family your phone belongs to.
- Your family's shared plan (member names, routines, tasks and notes) and tick history (which task was done, on which
  day, and when) are stored with our database provider, Supabase, so every phone in the family can see them.
- Only phones that joined with your family code can read this information.
- Data is encrypted in transit.

Voice recordings, settings and the backup text are not synced.

**Leaving and deleting.** "Leave the family" in Settings removes your phone from the family. To have your family's
synced data deleted entirely, email us at [contact email] with your family code. We delete it within 30 days.

## What we don't do

- We don't sell or rent your information.
- We don't show ads in the app.
- We don't use analytics or tracking tools in the app.
- We don't use your information to build advertising profiles.

## Children

Akol is designed for parents and carers to set up for their families. A parent can add a child's first name and
routines. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Children's names stay on the phone unless the
parent turns on family sync, and then they are shared only with the family's own phones. A parent can ask us to delete
synced family data at any time (see above).

## Permissions the app asks for

- **Notifications**, for timed reminders and alarms.
- **Microphone**, only when you choose to record a voice alarm.
- **Exact alarms** (Android), so reminders fire on the minute.
