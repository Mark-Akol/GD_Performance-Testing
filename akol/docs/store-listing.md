## Description (both stores)

> **Your routine, just a-call away.**
>
> Akol turns your day into a timed checklist, for you on your own or for the whole family. 6:45 pack lunch. 6:55
> bags in the car. 7:00 tune the GPS. 7:30: Go time. Has everyone done everything?
>
> **ON TIME, EVERY TIME**
> • Every task has a time, and Akol reminds you on the minute.
> • Set the must-dos to Alarm. They ring through Focus and Do Not Disturb, and keep ringing until someone ticks them.
> • Voice alarms: record "Hey Ra, it's time to go!" in your own voice. If a task still isn't done, that's what plays.
>
> **ONE LIST, OR THE WHOLE CREW**
> • A checklist for each person, plus a Whole Crew view with everyone's tasks on one timeline.
> • Go time checkpoints gather everything still open across the family before you head out.
> • Family sync: ticks on one phone show up on every phone, straight away. No accounts, no passwords, just a family
>   code.
>
> **EASY**
> • Routines for school mornings, weekends, bedtime: you choose the days.
> • One-off tasks for today, tomorrow or any date: dentist, parcel, school trip money.
> • Streaks and progress for the kids.
> • Backup in one tap, restore on a new phone.
>
> **PRIVATE**
> No ads. No tracking. Your routines stay on your phone unless you turn on family sync.

## Privacy answers

**Apple App Privacy ("nutrition label")**
- Data collected: **User Content (Other)** (family plan and ticks, only with family sync) and **Identifiers (User ID)**
  (anonymous sign-in, only with family sync).
- Purpose: App Functionality. Linked to the user: No (no name, email or phone). Used for tracking: No.
- Everything else: not collected.

**Google Play Data safety**
- Collects: **App activity → Other actions** (ticks) and **Personal info → Other** (family member names), only with
  family sync. Purpose: App functionality. Not shared with third parties.
- Encrypted in transit: Yes. Users can request deletion: Yes (by email, see the privacy policy).
- Exact alarm declaration: "User-set timed reminders and alarms."

Revisit both answers before adding any ad or measurement tools (TikTok, Google, Meta).
